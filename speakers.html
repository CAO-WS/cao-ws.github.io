Panel

The world changed. Should the model?

When conditions change after deployment, changing the model is not always the right response. Sometimes adaptation is necessary; sometimes stability, abstention, rollback, routing, or human intervention is safer. The panel brings together perspectives from learning under uncertainty, frontier-model evaluation, AI infrastructure, and real-world product deployment to discuss what makes a good change, when systems should stay stable, and how to tell whether an intervention actually improved reliability.
