Taking part

In person, with room for exceptions

CAO 2027 is an in-person workshop, and we strongly encourage speakers, authors, and participants to attend. If visa, accessibility, or unavoidable travel constraints keep you away, and where ICLR permits, an on-site organizer or volunteer can display your poster and relay questions, and you can share a pre-recorded presentation for the website.

Discussion before, during, and after

Subject to ICLR approval, we will run a workshop Discord with spaces for accepted papers, the three pillars, the Failure Clinic, open problems, and introductions. We will also use the discussion features of the official ICLR platform where available. Organizers and student volunteers will moderate and bring questions into the room.

Support for students

We plan to support three student volunteers, covering their registration and giving them hands-on experience alongside the organizing team. CAO 2026 gave ten financial awards to participants; for 2027 we plan to increase financial assistance, prioritizing students, first-time attendees, researchers from under-resourced institutions, underrepresented groups, and anyone facing financial or geographic barriers.

Awards

Best Paper and Best Poster awards recognize especially strong contributed work, including work by junior researchers.

Accessibility

An organizer will serve as accessibility contact and coordinate accommodations with the conference. With author consent and subject to ICLR policy, accepted papers, posters, and talk materials will be shared on this site.
